DE-LU day-ahead electricity prices, hourly averages, one file per year.

Source : Bundesnetzagentur / SMARD, retrieved via api.energy-charts.info
License: CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0)
Note   : the German day-ahead auction moved to 15-minute market time units
         during 2025; sub-hourly values were averaged to hourly.

Annual means (EUR/MWh): 2022 235.4 | 2023 95.2 | 2024 78.5 | 2025 89.3

spot_DE_LU_2025.csv is the series used by the paper, via
make_spot2025_prices.py -> Munich_DH_data_spot2025.xlsx.
